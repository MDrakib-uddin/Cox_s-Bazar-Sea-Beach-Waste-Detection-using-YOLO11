Plastic pollution on coastal areas is a growing environmental concern, especially in popular tourist destinations like Cox’s Bazar. The accumulation of plastic waste negatively impacts marine life, local ecosystems, and human health. This project aims to automatically detect and classify plastic waste on the beach using Deep Learning techniques, specifically YOLOv8 object detection.
